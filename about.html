Our Story & Mission

Empowering Teams to Build Without Operational Drag

We founded Stackly with a single thesis: ambitious technology companies spend too much time orchestrating tools rather than innovating products.

The Origin

Born from Engineering Frustration

In 2023, our founders were leading engineering and product at fast-scaling SaaS companies. Every day was lost to status update pings, manually updating tickets, and reconciling disconnected metrics across four different dashboards.

We envisioned a synchronized platform where business intelligence, project backlogs, and automations live in a unified database. Today, Stackly serves over 25,000+ teams across 80+ countries.

"We built Stackly to eliminate operational drag so software builders can focus on shipping products that matter."

Alexandre Keller, Co-Founder & CEO
